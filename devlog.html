DEV LOG.

Pushinn is built in the open. Here is what we've built, newest first, in plain words.

Week of 26 OctNext build 6 changes
  • NEW Chat is back, with Report and Block on every message.
  • NEW Report clips, posts and profiles straight from the app.
  • FIXED Auto check-out keeps working after you leave the spot screen.
  • FIXED If something won't load (feed, map, battles, rewards), you now get a clear message and a Retry button instead of an empty screen.
  • FIXED 'Mark all as read' actually marks your notifications read.
  • NEW Ad privacy choices and map credits in Settings.
Week of 19 Oct 13 changes
  • NEW Trim your video before you post it.
  • NEW Pick a cover frame for your clip, and change it after posting.
  • NEW Full-screen clip viewer. Landscape clips turn sideways.
  • NEW Swipe up and down through a skater's clips on their profile.
  • FIXED Big videos get shrunk to fit instead of refused.
  • NEW Report a bug from Settings.
  • FIXED The app asks for your location once, and explains why first.
  • SAFER Battle votes stay sealed until both skaters have voted.
  • SAFER Live sessions can only be joined with the code.
  • SAFER Blocking someone now covers comments, likes, invites and notifications.
  • SAFER No lookalike or fake staff usernames.
  • SAFER Chat photos can only be opened by people in that chat.
  • FIXED Videos pause when you switch tab.
Week of 12 Oct 3 changes
  • NEW Redesigned post screen.
  • SAFER Battle clips have to be your own upload for that battle.
  • SAFER Trick scores are worked out by Pushinn, so they can't be faked.
Week of 5 Oct 1 change
  • NEW Delete your account any time. Your battles stay as 'Deleted skater' and your spots stay on the map.
