Core Systems

Territorial playbooks for the street-level network.

PushInn turns local sessions into a live municipal control layer built on battle logic, geospatial intelligence, and a permanent street archive.

01

The Borough Battle Engine

Stop chasing follower counts. Challenge local riders to timed trick battles and S.K.A.T.E. matchups. Every skate clip you upload and every battle you win contributes to your crew’s dominance over legally recognized municipal boundaries like Camden or Southwark. Capture the borough and stamp the map with your crew's colors.

02

Geospatial Intelligence

Explore a live map powered by real-time environmental logging. Beyond just spot locations, get active intelligence on pavement quality, lingering rain, and active security patrols. Log spot status to earn XP and keep the local scene one step ahead of the heat.

03

The Solo Destabilizer

Operate as an independent ghost. Even without a crew, your anonymous skate clips carry structural weight. High solo activity "destabilizes" rival territories, fading their color opacity on the map and signaling to the city that their neighborhood is ready for a takeover.

04

The Grind Archive

Every spot has a history; now it has an immutable ledger. Pushinn functions as a community-driven archive, cataloging every battle and skate clip ever recorded at a map node. Whether a spot has ten years of legacy or just surfaced last week, new riders can pull up and instantly see the tricks that went down before them, preserving the heritage of the streets for the next generation.

05

The Scouting Dashboard

Your activity heatmap and battle stats build a dynamic player card. Verified brands and local shops use the backend dashboard to scout raw street talent, issue gear stipends, and recruit riders directly based on objective skill and territorial dominance.
