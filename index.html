The Setup

How it works on the ground.

01

Street Battles

Challenge locals to S.K.A.T.E. or timed trick battles. Win to claim your ends and put your crew's stamp on the map.

02

Spot Intel

See which spots are actually skateable right now. Get heads-up on pavement quality, rain, and whether the spot is bust-free before you head out.

03

Private Profile

Keep your sessions private when you need to. You can build up your reputation without having to be a public figure on the app.

04

The Grind Archive

An immutable ledger of street history. See the tricks that went down at every map node, preserving the heritage of the streets for the next generation.

05

The Scouting Dashboard

Verified brands and shops use your dynamic player card to scout talent, issue gear stipends, and recruit riders based on objective skill and territorial dominance.

06

Location Privacy

Granular control over your data. Choose exactly how much location data to reveal, from full coordinates to controlled or anonymous sharing.
