System Briefing

TRUSTLESS_COMPETITION_DOCUMENTATION

1. Core Identity

PushInn is “Strava meets Chess.com for skateboarding”: a geospatial social ecosystem where skill is proven through community consensus, not vanity metrics.

2. Tribal Verification Protocol

[ONBOARDING_NOTE]: Verification access is coordinated privately through approved onboarding channels to protect rider trust and platform integrity.

To protect trust, onboarding uses a high-friction gate: a 24-hour third-party clip submission or a cryptographic invite token from an already verified rider.

3. Asynchronous Battle Engine

Battle mode is built for timezone-safe competition with turn-state handling, timeout/forfeit logic, and democratic outcome verification for S.K.A.T.E., S.K.8, and custom formats.

4. Triple-Score Metrics

Every rider is measured through Map Score (utility), Player Score (skill), and Ranking Score (trust). These metrics drive verification weight and sponsor readiness.

5. Heat Alerts & Spot Governance

Live map intel accepts Wet, Dry, Locked Off, Security Active, and Session Alive reports on a 4-hour TTL. Privacy tiers support Public, Crew, and Invite-only spot visibility.
