Pushinn Safety Standards

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy for CSAM

Pushinn maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and the sexual exploitation of minors. We are committed to keeping our community safe and strictly prohibit any content that endangers children.

Content Moderation & In-App Reporting

Pushinn actively monitors public feeds and allows users to flag and report inappropriate content directly within the application. Any content or behavior that violates child safety laws will result in immediate account termination.

Compliance & Law Enforcement

We comply with all relevant child safety laws. If CSAM or related illegal activities are detected or reported, we will immediately preserve the evidence, terminate the offending accounts, and report the incident to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant national and international law enforcement authorities.

Contact

If you have any safety concerns or need to report an incident, please contact our designated compliance officer at: admin@pushinn.app.
