Pushinn Safety Standards

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy for CSAM

Pushinn maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and the sexual exploitation of minors. We are committed to keeping our community safe and strictly prohibit any content that endangers children.

Content Moderation & In-App Reporting

Users can report posts directly within the app, and we review every report. Any content or behavior that violates child safety laws will result in immediate account termination.

Compliance & Law Enforcement

We comply with all relevant child safety laws. If CSAM or related illegal activities are detected or reported, we will immediately preserve the evidence, terminate the offending accounts, and report the incident to the UK National Crime Agency (CEOP) and the Internet Watch Foundation.

Contact

If you have any safety concerns or need to report an incident, please contact our child safety contact at: admin@pushinn.app.
