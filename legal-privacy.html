Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: May 2026. Your data autonomy is a core protocol requirement.

01 | Data Autonomy

We collect only the data necessary to verify trick performance and spot heat. You maintain full ownership of your media uploads.

02 | Location Privacy (Compliance Clause)

PushInn uses background location data for **Geospatial Verification**—confirming your presence at a map node during a battle or spot log.

- Location data is encrypted in transit and at rest. - We do not sell your location history to third parties. - You can choose 'Incognito Mode' to destabilize territories without revealing your precise GPS coordinates to other users.

03 | Community Moderation

UGC (User Generated Content) is monitored by the community ledger. Misleading, harassing, or illegal content is flagged and purged. Accounts associated with such content will be decommissioned.

04 | Account Deletion

Users can request immediate and permanent data purging via the Account Deletion tool.
