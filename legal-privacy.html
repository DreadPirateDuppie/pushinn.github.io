Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

Pushinn collects data to verify user performance at skate and BMX spots. This includes account information provided during registration and user-generated content (media uploads).

2. Location Privacy

Pushinn utilizes background location services to perform Geospatial Verification. This confirms that users are physically present at map nodes when logging a spot or participating in a battle. Location data is stored using industry-standard encryption, is not sold to third parties, and users may revoke access or enable 'Incognito Mode' within the application settings at any time.

3. Moderation and Safety

User-generated content (UGC) is subject to community moderation. Pushinn employs a community-led reporting system. Harassment, bullying, or the upload of illegal/harmful content is strictly prohibited. Pushinn reserves the right to decommission accounts that violate these safety standards.

4. Data Deletion

Users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated data. Requests can be submitted via the support portal at support.html#delete. Please include your username and the email address associated with your account in your request.
