PROTOCOL ROADMAP

Architecture & Vision.

The phased operational plan for Pushinn: bridging London street skateboarding culture with decentralized civic ownership and community-voted public goods funding.

MILESTONE 01

Phase 1: Street Foundation

Live Beta

Establishing the primary consumer mobile app, skater discovery loops, and local community interaction across London.

> Spot Discovery Engine

Curated, skater-mapped database of street spots, parks, and DIY terrain with granular media verification and anti-duplicate coordinates.

> Territorial Heat

Live London borough activity monitoring, trackable session frequency, and localized skater turf engagement.

> P2P Versus Battles

Server-authoritative game of S.K.A.T.E. wagering engine protected by atomic database row locks and community dispute review.

> Anti-Inflation Rewards

Controlled in-app economy replacing infinite point printing with daily streak Loot Boxes and tiered probability payouts.

MILESTONE 02

Phase 2: Subculture & Commerce

Upcoming

Deepening integration with London’s independent retail network and physical skate institutions.

> Independent Skate Shop Network

Merchant portal connecting independent brick-and-mortar skate shops directly into the app ecosystem for foot-traffic activation.

> Spot Sponsorships & Bounties

Local shops and brands sponsoring specific spots, trick bounties, and community sessions with tangible physical rewards.

> Gear & Hardware Perks

Redeeming in-app progress for local shop discounts, hardware restocks, and exclusive event access.

> Community Media Showcases

Integrated municipal video premieres and skater spotlight clips verified directly on the platform feed.

MILESTONE 03

Phase 3: The Protocol & Civic Treasury

Web3 Protocol Spec

Transitioning the validated mobile ecosystem into a sovereign token economy designed to fund real-world skateboarding infrastructure.

> Fixed Supply & Proof-of-Skate

Sovereign cryptographic token deployment with 60%+ allocation reserved strictly for verified skaters discovering spots, logging sessions, and winning battles.

> The Community Treasury (Civic Pot)

Instead of discarding battle fees via artificial deflationary token burns, transaction fees flow into an accumulated community treasury pool.

> DIY Skatepark Repairs & Upkeep

Decentralized treasury capital mobilized directly to pay for concrete repairs, DIY obstacle builds, and upkeep at local London skate parks.

> Grassroots Creative Voting

Community governance enabling token holders to vote on funding independent video parts, skate zines, photo exhibitions, and artist collaborations.
