01 // THE MISSION

BUILT FOR SKATERS. ROOTED IN THE STREETS.

Skateboarding drives immense global culture, fashion, and media, but the street scene has never had dedicated digital infrastructure built specifically for the culture. Pushinn gives skaters verified spot radar, permanent progression archives, and community-directed funding.

THE CURRENT REALITY

Fragmented & Ephemeral

  • • Spot knowledge and session meetups live trapped in disappearing group chats and scattered DMs.
  • • Raw street clips get lost in algorithmic feeds with zero permanent trick archive or spot leaderboards.
  • • Talented street skaters lack verified athlete resumes to showcase battle records and land sponsorship.
THE PUSHINN FOUNDATION

Unified & Permanent

  • ✓ Real-time spot radar, active session check-ins, and on-site trick clips pinned to exact obstacles.
  • ✓ The permanent Trick Archive (NBD records) and verified skater resume cards for industry recognition.
  • ✓ The Digital Susu: A creative community treasury funding skater DIY projects, films, and zines directly.

03 // CAREER ENGINE

THE SKATER CARD: A VERIFIABLE CAREER PASSPORT

Skaters currently have no formal athlete portfolio. When seeking sponsorships, they send chaotic Instagram DMs with fragmented footage. Pushinn turns your street history into a verified athletic resume.

01 // VERIFIED TRICK INDEX

Proof of Make

Every landed trick logged, GPS-verified, and peer-reviewed by the community. Zero fake claims or stolen clips.

02 // S.K.A.T.E. WIN RECORD

Battle Stats

Public win/loss records, letters defended, and competitive rating so sponsors can see real head-to-head consistency.

03 // PRO SCOUTING PORTAL

Industry Outreach

One clean link that brands and local skate shops can audit when signing team riders, dropping product flow, or funding tours.

04 // COMMUNITY CAPITAL

THE DIGITAL SUSU: MARKETING REINVESTED IN SKATERS

Inspired by traditional community rotating savings associations (Susu/Pardner), Pushinn treats our growth budget not as ad waste, but as capital to empower the creative ideas of our own scene.

01

Community Pitch

Skaters propose creative projects: pouring concrete at a DIY spot, filming an underground street video, or printing a scene zine.

02

Skaters Deliberate

The community votes transparently on which proposals receive funding. Skaters decide where their scene's capital goes.

03

Real Cultural Uplift

Pushinn pays the creators and helps execute the project as our authentic marketing spend. Real art is made; real skaters get paid.

05 // THE ECONOMIC FLYWHEEL

THE AUTONOMOUS SKATE ECONOMY

How Pushinn scales beyond an app into a thriving, self-sustaining financial network for the culture.

SPONSORSHIPS REDEFINED

Brand Bounties on Spots

Brands and local skate shops drop bounties directly onto spot pins: "Land a switch hardflip down the 5-stair and claim a sponsor bounty or a fresh deck from the local shop." Immediate economic incentive for raw street progression.

SUPPORTING CORE SHOPS

Skate Shop Flywheel

Independent core skate shops become physical community hubs where app rep and points redeem for physical decks, wheels, hardware, and shop apparel. Keeping money inside the independent ecosystem.

TERRITORIAL COMPETITION

Borough Turf Wars

Crews compete for neighborhood dominance across entire London boroughs (Southwark, Camden, Hackney), claiming spots through session check-ins, battle win streaks, and trick density.

FAN ENGAGEMENT

Fantasy Skate Leagues

Followers and fans draft street skaters onto fantasy rosters, earning points based on real-world S.K.A.T.E. battle win rates, NBD archive claims, and session frequency.
