Closed Beta

Get on the tester list.

Welcome to the Pushinn closed beta. You're getting the app early, in its infancy, before it's public on the App Store and Google Play.

Once there's a build ready, you'll receive an email with a link to install it — TestFlight if you're on iPhone, Google Play if you're on Android.

As a tester you're helping with two things. One is catching bugs — anything that crashes, breaks, or behaves in a way it shouldn't. The other matters just as much: helping me refine what the app actually feels like to use. So I don't only want to hear what's broken and what's working — I want to know what you'd like to see more of, what feels off, and where you think the experience could be better.

Everyone who tests and gives feedback keeps Pushinn Premium free, permanently, from the day the app launches. Nothing to pay now or later.

iPhone testers go through TestFlight. Android testers go through Google Play.

Helps me chase down layout bugs on your exact screen.

Important: use the email attached to your Google Play account (Android) or your Apple ID (iPhone). The invite won't work on any other address.

Beta places are limited. Testers get in first and stay free.
